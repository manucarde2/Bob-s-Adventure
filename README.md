🧸 A little guy. An adventure. No real reason.

Bob is a simple little guy. One day, bored of the usual routine, he decides—for no particular reason—to go on an adventure.

Join Bob on a surreal journey through grassy biomes, sun-soaked deserts, and dark caves, where he will encounter bizarre enemies and discover amazing abilities.

Explore, jump, survive… or just enjoy watching him fall spectacularly.  
The world is strange, and Bob is no less strange than it.

---

## 🎮 How the game works

Bob is a simple and straightforward pixel art platformer, but full of challenges.  
Along the way, you will find blocks containing power-ups that grant Bob special abilities to overcome obstacles and defeat enemies.

### 🔹 Available power-ups

- **Speed** – Bob runs much faster  
- **Fight** – Unlocks punches to hit nearby enemies  
- **Fire** – Allows Bob to spit fireballs  
- **Flight** – Bob can glide through the air to reach distant platforms

---

## 👾 Enemies to face

- **Loomby** – Small yellow creatures you can squash by jumping on them  
- **Flarby** – Green flying spheres moving left and right  
- **Cannons** – Mounted on rails, shooting cannonballs  
- **Dodondo** – Giant creatures that spit Loomby from their mouths

---

## 🎮 Controls

- **Right/Left arrow keys** – Move right and left  
- **Z** – Jump  
- **X** – Use a special ability (if you have a power-up)  
- **Esc** – Pause the game
